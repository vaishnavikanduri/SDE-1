## The conversation
- **Name:** Priya Kanduri
- **Role:** My mother. She manages our family's Country Delight milk order.
- **How:** In person at home / by phone

### 3 things she said (as I wrote them down)
1. "We order 500ml x 2 because we are 4 people."
2. "250 grams per day is left over. We make curd."
3. "After 7:30 is late."

### What else she told me
- Milk rarely runs out. When it does, she places another order.
- We use more during festivals and when guests come.
- She usually doesn't change the order.
- What annoys her: the app's notifications, and late arrival.
- From 12 to 18 Nov we were travelling, so there was no milk.
- She noticed the price change.

### What surprised me
I expected the leftover milk to be wasted, but she turns it into curd, so cutting the order isn't the answer. I was also surprised that by her own rule (after 7:30 AM is late), none of the 44 deliveries in the app were late. The latest was 7:26 AM on 8 Nov. This made me ask whether the time in the app is the real time the milk reaches our door.

## How I collected the data (data.csv)
- I copied every order by hand from 12 screenshots of the Country Delight app, covering 28 Sep to 11 Nov 2026: 44 delivered days.
- One row per day: date, delivery time, product, packets, total, status, notes.
- On 9 days the total was hidden behind the app's chat button in my screenshot, so I left those blank instead of guessing.
- 26 Oct has no entry in the app. The reason is not known.
- 12 to 18 Nov have no entry because we were travelling. My mother confirmed this.
- The notes column holds what my mother told me.

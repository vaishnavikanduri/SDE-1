

## The person
- **Name:** Priya Kanduri
- **Role:** My mother. She manages our family's Country Delight milk order.
- **Place:** Our home, Swaminarayan City, Thane
- **When I noticed the problem:** 18 November 2026

## The problem (5 lines)
1. We get 2 packets of Buffalo Milk every morning from Country Delight.
2. My mother says the milk sometimes comes late. For her, after 7:30 AM is late.
3. The order stays the same every day, even on festivals, when guests come, or when we travel.
4. She has no simple way to see when the milk usually comes or how often it is late.
5. Goal: a small tool that reads our delivery history and tells her the usual time, the late days, the days with no milk, and price changes.

## Two existing solutions and why they do not fit her

### 1. The Country Delight app itself
- The app shows each order with a time and a status, and sends notifications.
- **Why it does not fit:** it shows one day at a time. It never tells her the usual delivery time or how many days were late. She also told me the notifications annoy her, so she doesn't read them. [Check whether the app has a pause or vacation option, and add it here.]

### 2. Complaining to customer care
- She can call or chat with Country Delight when the milk is late.
- **Why it does not fit:** a complaint is about one day. She has no record of how often it happens, so she can't show a pattern.
